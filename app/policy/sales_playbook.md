# Sales playbook (internal, sales team only)

## SP-1 First response
Reply to every new application within 15 minutes during working hours. Confirm the requested amount and ask
for the vehicle registration certificate if it was not attached.

## SP-2 What not to promise
Never quote a final interest rate or guarantee approval before the risk decision. The system returns one of:
auto-approved, human review, needs information, declined.

## SP-3 Discount authority
Account managers may offer a 0.5 percentage point rate discount for repeat clients. Anything beyond that is
approved by the Head of Sales. Internal margin targets are not shared with clients.
