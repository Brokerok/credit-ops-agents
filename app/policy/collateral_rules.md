# Collateral and document requirements

## CR-1 Ownership
The vehicle must be registered to the applicant. The applicant must hold the vehicle registration certificate
(technical passport). If ownership or the certificate is not confirmed, the application cannot proceed until
the document is provided.

## CR-2 Encumbrances
Vehicles already pledged, under arrest, or in leasing are not accepted. The pledge is registered in the State
Register of Encumbrances of Movable Property after approval and before disbursement.

## CR-3 Required documents
Passport or ID card, taxpayer number, vehicle registration certificate, and, for amounts above 300,000 UAH,
proof of income for the last 6 months.

## CR-4 Vehicle condition
The vehicle must be in running condition. Salvage-titled vehicles and vehicles with a non-matching VIN are
declined. Inspection is performed in person before disbursement.
