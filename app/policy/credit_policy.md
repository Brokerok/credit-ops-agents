# Credit Policy for vehicle-secured consumer loans

## CP-1 Scope
This policy applies to all consumer loans where the collateral is a passenger car or light commercial vehicle
owned by the applicant. It defines the limits that an automated pre-screening may apply without a credit
officer and the cases that must go to a human reviewer.

## CP-2 Loan amount and term
The minimum loan amount is 20,000 UAH and the maximum is 2,000,000 UAH. Terms from 3 to 36 months are
standard; terms of 37 to 60 months require approval by the Head of Risk. Requests outside the amount range
are declined automatically.

## CP-3.1 Vehicle valuation
The collateral value is the estimated market value produced by the valuation service, not the value declared
by the applicant. If the declared value exceeds the estimate by more than 25%, the application is flagged for
review because the applicant's expectations are unlikely to be met.

## CP-3.2 Maximum loan-to-value (LTV)
The loan amount may not exceed 60% of the estimated vehicle value (LTV ≤ 60%) for automated approval.
Applications with LTV above 60% and up to 70% go to human review. Applications with LTV above 70% are declined.

## CP-4 Vehicle age and mileage
Vehicles older than 15 years at the date of application are not accepted as collateral. Vehicles between 12 and
15 years old are accepted only after human review. Mileage above 300,000 km requires human review regardless
of age.

## CP-5 Affordability
The applicant's declared monthly income must be at least 2 times the estimated monthly payment. The
estimated monthly payment is principal divided by term plus 3% monthly interest on the principal. If income is
not declared the application cannot be approved automatically and must request the missing information.

## CP-6 Automated approval limits
Automated approval is allowed only when every rule in CP-2 to CP-5 passes and the requested amount does not
exceed 300,000 UAH. Larger amounts always go to a credit officer, even if all rules pass.

## CP-7 Decision authority
The automated system may: decline on hard rules (CP-2 amount range, CP-3.2 LTV above 70%, CP-4 age above
15 years), approve within CP-6 limits, request missing information, or route to human review. It may not
override a failed rule, change limits, or promise terms to an applicant.
